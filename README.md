# RoverVilla

> **A digital memorial for the machines we left behind on Mars and the Moon.**
> 
> *Their mission ended. Their story didn't.*

RoverVilla introduces young space enthusiasts to the machines left on Mars and the Moon through illustrated stories, comics, storybooks, interactive hardware, and mission-inspired games. Source-linked facts and evidence sit beneath the storytelling layer.

**Explore:** [rovervilla.ahmedtrooper.me](https://rovervilla.ahmedtrooper.me/)
